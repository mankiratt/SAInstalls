ABOUT US

The people behind the joinery.

Hallam-based, Melbourne-wide, and hands-on with every project we take on.

OUR STORY

30 years of craft.
A lifetime of dedication.

S&A Joinery was built on a simple belief — that every home deserves quality craftsmanship that stands the test of time. As Melbourne cabinet makers with more than 30 years of experience, we bring unmatched knowledge and skill to every project we take on.

Every piece we build is handcrafted to your exact specifications. We use only premium materials and hardware because we believe the details matter. From the first consultation to the final installation, we treat every home like our own.

Based in Hallam, we're the local cabinet maker of choice for homeowners right across south-east Melbourne — from Berwick, Clyde North, Cranbourne, Narre Warren and Pakenham through to Glen Waverley, the inner city and the eastern suburbs.
